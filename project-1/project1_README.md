# UI/UX Design Internship — Project 1: Empathy Mapping
**DecodeLabs | Batch 2026**

## 📌 Overview
This repository contains my submission for **Project 1** of the UI/UX Design Internship at DecodeLabs. The task focused on **user empathy and research synthesis** — understanding a target user's thoughts, feelings, actions, and pain points before any design work begins.

## 🎯 Scenario
Designing a fitness app for busy office professionals who struggle to find time to exercise. The goal was to build an Empathy Map for a representative user, capturing what she **says, thinks, does, and feels**, in order to uncover an actionable design insight.

## 👤 Persona Snapshot
**Amna Khan** — Age 29, Marketing Executive
**Target Audience:** Office professionals, 25–40, working 40+ hour weeks

She wants stress relief and mental clarity but faces constant time pressure, exhaustion, and unrealistic fitness app expectations.

## 🧠 Empathy Map Breakdown
- **Says:** "I just don't have time to work out." / "I'll start next week." / "I'm too exhausted by the time I get home."
- **Thinks:** "I'm falling behind my peers." / "I feel unhealthy."
- **Does:** Drinks 3–4 coffees, skips lunch, sits 8+ hours, has a passive commute.
- **Feels:** Guilt, burnout, physical stiffness, anxiety.

## 💡 Key Insight
> Because Amna feels guilty and physically drained but has no spare time, she needs an app built around **short, desk-friendly micro-workouts** — not long gym sessions or loud, gamified experiences.

This insight directly informed the design direction carried into later project milestones.
